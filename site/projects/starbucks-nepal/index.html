03 · Nepal's economy

Modest growth, and an economy that leans on remittances

The indicators below come from the World Bank. They describe the setting a market entry would face and do not forecast café demand.

Growth against inflation

Per cent. Growth slows to 2.3% in FY2026 while inflation is projected to rise. FY2027 growth is the FY2027 to FY2028 average.

GDP growthInflation

Why this matters for entry

What the numbers suggest for a premium café brand.

Slower growth. Projected GDP growth falls from 4.4% to 2.3% in FY2026, so the near-term picture is weaker than 2025.
Rising prices. Inflation is projected at 4.3% then 5.1%, which can squeeze household spending.
Remittance reliance. Remittances are 26% of GDP, so household income depends on conditions abroad.
Currency link. The rupee is pegged to the Indian rupee, so Nepal's exchange rate follows India's.

Source: World Bank Nepal Development Update (April 2026) and World Bank Open Data. Tourist arrivals from the Nepal Tourism Board.

04 · Entry mode

Control against exposure: four ways in

The four modes are rated on control, capability transfer, local knowledge, risk sharing and capital exposure. The joint venture is the one recommended for evaluation.

The four modes side by side

Green is favourable to Starbucks, amber is mixed, red is unfavourable. For control and capital exposure, favourable means high control and low exposure.

Precedent. The joint venture mirrors Starbucks' India JV (Fischer and Roy, 2017) and the 2026 China restructuring. It is a recommendation to evaluate, not a decision backed by Nepal demand data.

05 · Rules of entry

Nepal's investment rules and what changed in 2026

The legal route into Nepal matters as much as the strategy. Retail is restricted, but there is an exception for international chains, and the automatic approval route was widened in February 2026.

Current lawFITTA 2019 and the Industrial Enterprises Act 2020The governing legislation for foreign investment.
RetailRestricted, with an exceptionRetail is restricted, but the restriction excludes international chain retail trading in two or more countries.
Local cateringClosed to foreign equityRelevant if a café were treated as catering rather than retail.
February 2026Automatic route widened102 industry areas, with the NPR 500m ceiling removed. Verified through legal commentary only; the primary Gazette text was not accessed.

What an application needs

Documents listed by the Department of Industries.

1. A project report
2. A joint venture agreement, if there is more than one investor
3. A Financial Credibility Certificate from the investor's home bank

A joint venture agreement would be needed under the recommended route, because a joint venture has more than one investor.

Source: Department of Industries (2026); Nepal Gazette notification of 16 February 2026 via Niti Partners and Mondaq commentary.

What is verified, and what is not

Hofstede scores

Long-term orientation and indulgence for Nepal excluded, not estimated.

Affordability

A ratio based on national GDP per capita was explored. GDP per capita is an average, not disposable income, and the figure could not be verified, so no affordability number is reported or used.
