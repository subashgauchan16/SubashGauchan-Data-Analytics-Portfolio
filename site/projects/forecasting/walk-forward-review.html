Supporting evidence · Exploratory prototype · Not a trading signal

Rebuilt forecast, tested year by year

The model predicts the 20-day change in price and is re-trained and tested one year at a time from 2015 to 2026, so every test year is unseen data. A Ridge regression on nine features against a rolling baseline.

Baseline (average move)Ridge model

Average error of the 20-day forecast, by test year

Mean absolute error of the predicted 20-day return, in percentage points. Lower is better. The two lines sit almost on top of each other, and both spike in 2020.

Share of 20-day moves called in the right direction, by test year

Higher is better. The baseline always says "up", so its score is simply how often markets rose.

Latest 20-day forecast against typical error

Forecast change from the close on 14 October 2026. The shaded band is the model's typical error for that instrument. Every forecast is far smaller than the typical error, so none of them is a usable directional call on its own.

Results by instrument, all test years

Average 20-day error in percentage points of price. BP and Shell are in pence on the London Stock Exchange, and the other five are index points. BP prices look dividend-adjusted and may be more volatile.
