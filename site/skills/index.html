MY WORKING PRINCIPLES

Good analysis is more than a clean chart.

01
Define the question

Start with a decision, not a visual.

02
Check the source

Look for missing values, anomalies and mismatched definitions.

03
Show the working

Make calculations and assumptions explainable.

04
State the limits

Separate observed results from inference and speculation.
