04 / HOW I LEARN

Learn the logic.
Build to understand.

I am largely self-taught, and I use AI-assisted development as a learning partner. I sometimes call it vibe coding, but for me the work starts with a problem, context and logic, not just a prompt.

I build practical tools and small games that help me get concepts through my head. CurioTrail, for example, turns SQL, Python, Excel and KPI analysis into interactive challenges I can practise and test.

AI can accelerate implementation, but its output still needs to be questioned. I focus on understanding the key logic, testing whether the behaviour matches the goal, investigating failures and being honest about limitations.
