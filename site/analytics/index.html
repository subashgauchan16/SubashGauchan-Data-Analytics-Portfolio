READ THE EVIDENCE

A result is only as strong as its definitions.

01

Source & scope

Check the dataset period, granularity, filters and exclusions before comparing values.

02

Metric logic

Revenue, profit and margin need clear formulas and consistent treatment of returns or missing records.

03

Interpretation

A dashboard highlights patterns; it does not by itself establish causation or prove a recommendation.

Open data questions: the 1,000 repeated rows may be genuine repeat orders or duplicates; there is no transaction ID to tell. 2014 and 2016 hold January to July only, so full-year comparisons with those years are not like-for-like. Currency and the definition of Profit are not documented in the source.
